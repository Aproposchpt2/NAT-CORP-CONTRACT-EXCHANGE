0–100 Match Rating

Understand the Match Before You Bid

The NAT-CORP match rating is a 0–100 decision-support indicator that helps contractors prioritize review. A higher rating signals stronger apparent alignment between the opportunity and the contractor’s confirmed business profile.

0–39Limited apparent alignment
40–59Review with caution
60–74Potential fit
75–89Strong review candidate
90–100Highest-priority review

Match ratings do not determine eligibility, replace solicitation review, or guarantee an award. Contractors must verify requirements in the official solicitation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers About NAT-CORP

Who is NAT-CORP for?

NAT-CORP is designed for all licensed contractors, including registered federal contractors, state-licensed and local contractors, suppliers, service providers, construction contractors, professional contractors, and other businesses qualified to pursue applicable public-sector opportunities.

What procurement markets are included?

The service supports validated opportunity sources from state agencies, counties, cities, municipalities, universities, colleges, school districts, hospitals, public authorities, and other supported public institutions.

Does NAT-CORP cover all 50 states?

No. NAT-CORP currently includes six states. Nationwide expansion is underway as additional public-sector publishers are onboarded and validated.

How much does the subscription cost?

The subscription begins with a 14-day free trial and is $119 per month after the trial. It is a recurring monthly subscription. You may cancel before the next billing date, and access continues through the current paid billing period.

What is the 0–100 match rating?

The match rating is a prioritization and decision-support indicator. It helps organize opportunity review but does not determine eligibility, replace the official solicitation, or guarantee an award.

What does an Analyze Fit Report cost?

An additional Analyze Fit Report costs $15 one-time for the selected opportunity. It provides deeper decision support beyond the dashboard match rating.

Is proposal development included?

No. Contract Proposal Development is a separately priced professional service with its own engagement scope and terms.

Is NAT-CORP a government agency?

No. NAT-CORP is an independent procurement-intelligence service operated by APROPOS Group LLC.
