Clear answers before you begin.

Why does today's contract marketplace create friction for businesses?

Public-sector opportunities are distributed across many websites, portals, jurisdictions, databases, and individual sources. Businesses may have to monitor multiple systems, interpret different terminology, screen large volumes of notices, and determine relevance before they can decide whether an opportunity is worth pursuing.

How does NAT-CORP start with my business?

NAT-CORP develops an evidence-backed Business Capability Profile based on what the business provides and uses that profile to identify available opportunities with meaningful capability alignment.

How can NAT-CORP extend Community Business Development services?

Community Business Development Agencies can continue providing counseling, mentoring, technical assistance, and business-development support while NAT-CORP adds a technology layer for business-centered contract opportunity discovery and evaluation.

Does NAT-CORP replace a business advisor or counselor?

No. NAT-CORP provides proactive opportunity intelligence. Human advisors and community organizations continue to provide the relationships, expertise, counseling, and guidance that businesses need.

What does NAT-CORP currently cover?

NAT-CORP currently supports six states, with nationwide expansion underway. Opportunity coverage varies by source availability and update schedules.

Does a match guarantee eligibility or award?

No. Businesses must verify all solicitation requirements on the official source and make their own pursuit decisions.

What does membership cost?

Start with a 14-day free trial. Continue for $119 per month, which includes unlimited contract discovery and downloads. An Analyze Fit Report is $79 one-time, purchased separately.
