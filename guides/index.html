What is SLED government contracting? SLED contracting covers procurement by state, local and education public entities. Unlike federal procurement, opportunities and vendor requirements are distributed across many jurisdictions and procurement systems. NAT-CORP focuses on matching businesses with state/local/SLED opportunities and helping them understand the correct procurement pathway.

NAT-CORP focuses on state and local public-sector opportunity matching. These guides explain the procurement environment businesses encounter across states, municipalities, counties, school systems and other eligible public entities.
