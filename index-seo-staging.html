Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use NAT-CORP?

NAT-CORP serves licensed contractors, including registered federal contractors seeking state and local public-sector opportunities.

What is included free?

A personalized dashboard, intelligent filtering, 0–100 estimated match ratings, five highest-rated current matches, and official procurement-source links.

Is Analyze Fit free?

No. The dashboard is free. Each optional Analyze Fit Report is $19.99.

Does a match guarantee eligibility or award?

No. Match ratings and reports provide decision support. Contractors must review the complete official solicitation and verify all requirements.
