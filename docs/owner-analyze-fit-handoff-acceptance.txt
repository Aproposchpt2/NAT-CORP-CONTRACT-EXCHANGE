Acceptance criteria:
1. Recording INTERESTED creates or reuses a contract-specific Analyze Fit service request.
2. The request metadata links contractor candidate, contract opportunity, and outreach event.
3. The owner receives an email containing the client-safe Analyze Fit URL.
4. The URL opens /analyze-fit-request with a scoped request ID and expiring opaque token.
5. Raw contractor and contract data are resolved server-side and are not embedded directly in the URL.
