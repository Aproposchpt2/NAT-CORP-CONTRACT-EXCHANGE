Where Arizona businesses start

Arizona Procurement Portal

The Arizona State Procurement Office identifies the Arizona Procurement Portal (APP) as the state's eProcurement system for sourcing, contracting and supplier management. Suppliers register in APP to participate in state solicitations.

Arizona State Procurement Office supplier guidance

Open state solicitations

Arizona's State Procurement Office directs suppliers to APP for current open solicitations. Supplier notifications are tied to the commodity codes selected in the vendor profile, making accurate capability classification important.

Beyond state agencies

Cities, counties, school systems and other public entities can create additional procurement markets. A complete opportunity strategy should distinguish state sourcing from local and institutional publishers.

Arizona government contracting FAQ

What is Arizona's state eProcurement system?

The Arizona State Procurement Office identifies the Arizona Procurement Portal (APP) as the eProcurement system used for state sourcing, contracting and supplier management.

Do suppliers need to register?

For State of Arizona procurement opportunities, the State Procurement Office instructs suppliers to complete APP supplier registration to participate in solicitations.

How does NAT-CORP help?

NAT-CORP is designed to reduce procurement-search fragmentation by organizing opportunities around business capabilities and supporting a structured review and fit-analysis workflow.
